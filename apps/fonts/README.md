# Atkinson Hyperlegible Next

The prompters' typeface, by the Braille Institute: its letterforms are drawn
to be told apart at a distance and at a glance, which is the whole job of a
prompter. Variable weight (200–800), Latin subset, from
[Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/fonts/atkinson-hyperlegible-next)
converted to TTF. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License (`OFL.txt`).

The Linux app embeds these files; the macOS app and the web prompter carry
their own copies, since a Swift package's resources and a crate's embedded
files must live inside them.
