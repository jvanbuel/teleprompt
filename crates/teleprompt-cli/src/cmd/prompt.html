Teleprompt

The take says this, not what the line reads. Keep it, and the line is reworded to match: nothing to record again.

Keys

Start a take from the line you are on; during one, keep it
EnterKeep the take
EscThrow the take away, or cancel the count
ZUndo the take just kept
PPause and resume
WReview a line said in other words
MMirror the text
+ −Text size
SHide or show the screen
Enter on a lineBetween takes, start a take from that line
F2Reword the line you are on, where it stands
SpaceRead by a voice: read aloud from the line you are on, or stop

For a voice that takes directions. Enter to apply.
