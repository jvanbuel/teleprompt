Keys

    

      
Start a take from the line you are on; during one, keep it

      
 EnterKeep the take

      
EscThrow the take away, or cancel the count

      
 ZUndo the take just kept

      
PPause and resume

      
WReview a line said in other words

      
MMirror the text

      
+ −Text size

      
SHide or show the screen

      
RRecord again, one after another, the lines reworded since their takes

      
VPlay the video as it will play, from the line you are on, or stop; ← → five seconds back or on

      
JPlay from what the last save moved

      
EEdit: drag a shot onto a word to start it there, or its grip to set how long it runs; hover a word to see the screen then

      
Enter on a lineBetween takes, start a take from that line

      
F2Reword the line you are on, where it stands

      
SpaceRead by a voice: play the video from the line you are on, or stop
